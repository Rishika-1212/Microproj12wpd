High-speed and large-scale intrinsically stretchable integrated circuits

New intrinsically stretchable electronics have been developed, offering high performance and integration. These include transistors with mobility exceeding 20 cm2/Vs under 100% strain, with a density of 100,000 transistors per cm2 and high drive current. A large-scale integrated circuit with over 1,000 transistors and a stage-switching frequency of over 1 MHz has been achieved. Additionally, a braille recognition system surpassing human skin sensing ability and a high-refresh-rate LED display have been demonstrated, enhancing the capabilities of skin-like electronics.

Penning micro-trap for quantum computing

A micro-fabricated Penning ion trap has been developed as an alternative to radio-frequency traps for quantum computing. This trap uses a 3 T magnetic field instead of radio frequencies, overcoming scaling challenges. It enables full quantum control of ions and arbitrary ion transport in the trapping plane above the chip. This innovation enhances connectivity and flexibility, advancing large-scale trapped-ion quantum computing, simulation, and sensing.

Integrated lithium niobate microwave photonic processing engine

Integrated microwave photonics (MWP) enables efficient generation, transmission, and manipulation of microwave signals in chip-scale optical systems. We've developed a high-speed MWP processing engine on a 4-inch wafer-scale thin-film lithium niobate platform. This engine performs tasks with processing bandwidths up to 67 GHz at low power CMOS-compatible voltages. It achieves ultrafast analogue computation, such as temporal integration and differentiation, at sampling rates up to 256 giga samples per second. We demonstrate three proof-of-concept applications: solving ordinary differential equations, generating ultra-wideband signals, and detecting edges in images. Additionally, we leverage the edge detector for medical image segmentation, outlining melanoma lesion boundaries effectively. This MWP engine offers compact, low-latency, and cost-effective solutions for wireless communications, radar, and photonic artificial intelligence.
